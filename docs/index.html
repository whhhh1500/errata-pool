ErrataPool

Staked bug bounties for AI benchmark answer keys, adjudicated by GenLayer validators.

How it works

  1. Maintainer funds a pool for a benchmark (an item-URL template with {id}), a reward per erratum and a minimum stake.
  2. Reviewer stakes on one item: wrong key (with the right letter), ambiguous, or flawed. The reward is reserved at filing.
  3. Validators fetch the item, normalise it identically, read the evidence and vote. They must agree on the item fingerprint, verdict and corrected key.
  4. Loser appeals once against the stored snapshot. Then anyone settles: reward + stake to the reviewer, or the stake goes into the pool. Accepted errata export as JSON.
