## What this demonstrates

* **Race protection.** While the burner's dialog for #11 was open, the owner settled #11 from another client. The burner then confirmed. The app re-read `get_report` and `preview_settle` right before signing, saw that the state had changed, and **sent nothing**. The harness then checked the app log and found no settle transaction from the burner.
* **The preview is what executes.** The amounts in the dialog come from the contract's `preview_settle` view. That view uses the same `_settle_plan` that `settle` runs. Report #12 settled as FORFEITED exactly as previewed.
* **Fail closed on RPC errors.** During the run, Studio's gateway twice answered without CORS headers. Once this hit `eth_sendRawTransaction`, and nothing was filed, so the harness retried. Once it hit the preview read, and the app logged *"Not sent: could not read the contract to prepare settle #12 … Nothing was signed; try again."* No dialog opened on stale data.
* **Pull payment.** #11 was settled by the owner's client, but the credit went to the reporter, the burner. The burner's Claim button showed the amount and its own claim dialog, and the claim transferred 1.5 GEN.

Three earlier attempts of this script (pools #3-#5) were cut short by the same Studio and Hugging Face infrastructure hiccups: a CORS-less gateway answer, and a 503 from the item host, which correctly reverted adjudication and left the report PENDING. They are recorded in `deployments/studionet.json` under `e2e_browser_aborted`. Their leftover reports #6-#9 were settled and pools #3-#5 closed afterwards. Report #10 is still PENDING and becomes recoverable by anyone at its deadline.
